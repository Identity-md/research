**How the evidence was checked**

- Discovery used public web searches for `$IMD`, `Identity-md`, `Identity.md`, `imd.fun`, and associated handles; indexed mirrors supplied additional post links and reply leads. Unrelated weather-department and other “IMD” results were excluded.
- Public X profiles were inspected for the initial candidates @surfcoderepeat, @taylor_, @blknoiz06, @0xminion, @yadfarms, @theneetguy, @Bankless, @Eli5defi, @AdamOnFinance, @wegocrypto88, @notwashed, and @nftimm. Reply discovery expanded the candidate set. This was a bounded discovery sample, not an exhaustive account census.
- For every table entry, the individual X post and its author's X profile were retrieved directly. The public server-rendered data supplied `Tweet.details.created_at_ms`, `User.verification.is_blue_verified`, and `User.relationship_counts.followers`. Reply or quote context was checked to establish relevance even where the author's own words do not contain “IMD.” Follower counts in the table come from the author's own profile response.
- Entries are ordered by publication time, not likes, follower count, mirror-relative age, or repost time. Multiple posts by one author are allowed. Bare reposts, mention-only replies, and emoji-only replies were excluded. Edited versions count once.
- The compact extracted evidence, source URLs, field names, exact timestamps, short excerpts, and context post IDs are delivered in `sources/evidence.json`. It is a research extraction, not a signed X archive. No dependency or network access is needed to read the deliverable.

**Uncertainty and unanswered questions**

The ten entries, timestamps, observed audience counts, and blue-check fields were substantiated from X's public responses. **Completeness remains unverified:** public search did not expose a usable live results feed, public profiles exposed only limited timeline samples, and the full reply trees were not paginated. Newer qualifying posts may exist outside the retrieved sample. Mirror caches also differed, so their relative ages were not used for final ordering.

Whether these are the ten most recent across *all* qualifying X accounts remains unanswered. The request also does not define “top”; a threshold of 100,000 followers, an engagement ranking, or exclusion of replies would produce a different list. This report makes its 10,000-follower interpretation explicit rather than presenting it as the requester's choice.

The claimed leaderboard results, agent autonomy, spending, and project adoption were not independently audited. Account relationships, sponsorship, and coordination were not established. A blue check and a large audience do not resolve those questions. No claim is made that these are ten independent endorsements or ten different authors.
