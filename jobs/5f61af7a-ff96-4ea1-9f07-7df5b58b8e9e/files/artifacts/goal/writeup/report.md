## Claim being investigated

The results identify the target as the monotone fan-in-2 AND/OR circuit complexity of the edge function for a bipartite graph on 26 vertices, with 13 vertices in each part and 51 edges. The edge set is the incidence graph of the projective plane of order 3 with edge `(0,13)` deleted. The reports use different cyclic index conventions; after converting them to the offset `right_index - left_index (mod 13)`, each gives offsets `{0,4,10,12}` with the deleted edge. The reported graph checks agree: 51 distinct edges; degree 3 at vertices 0 and 13 and degree 4 at the other vertices; no 4-cycle; and a matching of size 13, implying minimum vertex-cover size 13. [Angle 1](.imd/reads/artifacts/item-angle-1) [Angle 2](.imd/reads/artifacts/item-angle-2) [Angle 3](.imd/reads/artifacts/item-angle-3) [Angle 4](.imd/reads/artifacts/item-angle-4)

A star construction gives a circuit with 63 gates: 38 gates to OR the incident line variables at the 13 left vertices, 13 AND gates to join each left variable to its star, and 12 OR gates to combine the stars. Thus the results support the upper bound of 63. The requested theorem is optimality: every circuit computing this function has at least 63 gates.
