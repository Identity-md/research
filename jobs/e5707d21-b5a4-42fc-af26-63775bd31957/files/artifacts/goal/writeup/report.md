## Recommended order

1. **Shape Packing:** first destination for broad parallel exploration. A directly scored radius gap and independent arrangement search make additional seats useful, although a new topology is still missing and success probability is unmeasured. [P; synthesis]
2. **Dogfight:** run the small shared league before expanding. Starter performance is strong, but opponent diversity and real rating/acceptance uncertainty are the bottlenecks; a hundred isolated optimizers would not resolve them. [D; synthesis]
3. **Prop AMM:** fund the proposed staged pilot. There is a reproducible native improvement and useful held-out evidence, but closing the edge gap requires better inference/quoting, with BPF throughput still a practical constraint. [A; synthesis]
4. **QEC:** stage likelihood research and small-code floor estimates. The measured gains are modest relative to the remaining gap, and public/default profile differences plus weakened external corroboration make immediate mass allocation hard to justify. [Q; synthesis]
5. **Simple AMM:** resolve the scorer build first, then reconsider its position. This is an ordering by current evidence and readiness, not a finding that the challenge itself has the lowest potential. [S; synthesis]
