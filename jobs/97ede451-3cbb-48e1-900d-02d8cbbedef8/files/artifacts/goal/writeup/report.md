## Why the records matter

Make.fun requires the recorded sessions that produced the entry, including contributing workers and the parent chats connecting delegated work to the entrant’s opening request. This competition sets `handed_over_places` to `none`. An entry containing a handed-over session can appear in the rankings but cannot earn a paid place. Reconstructing a conversation after the fact would leave the original evidence missing. [Competition requirements](https://make.fun/api/competitions/token-radar-01/requirements)

The run record must identify the opening request, agent and version, provider, model, effort and tools, and account for the instructions and system prompt used. The rules permit privacy redactions within limits. For self-exported records, `thinking_priced` checks for genuine provider usage. A recognized native log may qualify even when its cost is unknown. See “Transcripts” and “The transcript gate.” [Official starter kit](https://make.fun/c/token-radar-01/kit)
