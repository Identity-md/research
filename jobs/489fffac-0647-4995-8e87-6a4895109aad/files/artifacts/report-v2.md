# Rivian Automotive, Inc. (NASDAQ: RIVN): company intelligence update

**Research date:** 2026-10-13 (Europe/Berlin)  
**Reporting cut-off:** public information available through 2026-10-13.  
**Question answered:** What can be established from attributable public records about Rivian Automotive, Inc.'s identity, leadership, operating position, financial condition, material legal/regulatory matters, and diligence gaps?

## How to read this report

Each item is deliberately labelled:

- **FACT** — directly supported by the linked source.
- **INFERENCE** — an analyst interpretation of stated facts; it is not a statement by Rivian or a prediction.
- **UNCERTAINTY / UNANSWERED** — a limit on what the reviewed public record establishes.

Amounts are USD and, unless stated otherwise, in millions. This is a research summary, not investment, legal, sanctions, or credit advice. A link is evidence for the specific assertion beside it; it is not an endorsement of every statement on the linked page.

## 1. Executive answer

- **FACT (high confidence):** Rivian is a Delaware corporation, CIK 0001874178, SEC file 001-41042, whose Class A common stock trades as **RIVN** on Nasdaq. Its latest filed periodic financial report as of the cut-off is the Form 10-Q for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, filed July 30, 2026. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, cover and filing detail](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000054/rivn-20260630.htm)
- **FACT (high confidence):** The company reported Q2 2026 revenue of $1.658bn, gross profit of $179m, and net loss of $837m. At June 30, it reported $3.592bn cash and cash equivalents, $1.718bn short-term investments, and $4.444bn carrying value of long-term debt. These interim statements are unaudited. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, statements and balance sheet](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000054/rivn-20260630.htm)
- **FACT (high confidence):** On October 2, Rivian reported Q3 production of 19,751 vehicles and deliveries of 19,248, and reaffirmed 2026 delivery guidance of 65,000–70,000. It said Q3 financial results would be released October 29; consequently, they were not public at this report's cut-off. [October 2, 2026 8-K exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000057/ex-9913q26deliveryproducti.htm)
- **INFERENCE (medium confidence):** The record supports a picture of an expanding but still loss-making EV manufacturer with a recent quarter of consolidated gross profit, material liquidity, and substantial execution and financing dependence. It does **not** establish sustainable profitability, because net losses continued, the latest quarterly results are unaudited, and R2 ramp, demand, cost, and funding remain material variables.
- **UNCERTAINTY (high confidence):** This report does not price the stock, estimate intrinsic value, or determine whether the company is an appropriate investment or counterparty. Those questions require current market data, contract-specific diligence, and (where relevant) beneficial-owner and sanctions screening.

## 2. Identity and business

- **FACT (high confidence):** Rivian Automotive, Inc. was incorporated in Delaware on March 26, 2015. The June 2026 10-Q gives its principal executive office as 14600 Myford Road, Irvine, California 92606, EIN 47-3544981, and website `www.rivian.com`. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, cover and Note 1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000054/rivn-20260630.htm)
- **FACT (high confidence):** Rivian describes itself as an American automotive technology company that develops and manufactures EVs, vertically integrated technologies, and value-added services; it reports two segments: Automotive and Software and Services. It says its vehicles are manufactured in the United States and sold directly to consumer and commercial customers. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Note 1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000054/rivn-20260630.htm)
- **FACT (high confidence):** Rivian said the Normal, Illinois factory's paint-shop upgrades enabled 215,000 annual units of production capacity and that customer deliveries of R2 began in Q2 2026. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, risk factors](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000054/rivn-20260630.htm)
- **INFERENCE (medium confidence):** The two-segment disclosure and R2 launch mean the company is pursuing a broader automotive-plus-software/services model, rather than relying solely on its existing vehicle lines. Whether software/services can become a higher-margin recurring revenue stream is a company expectation, not an independently verified outcome.

## 3. Leadership and governance

- **FACT (high confidence):** The April 2026 proxy identifies Robert J. Scaringe as Founder and CEO; Claire McDonough as CFO; and Michael Callahan as Chief Administrative Officer and Chief Legal Officer. It identifies Scaringe as Chairman of the Board. [2026 definitive proxy statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000023/rivn-20260427.htm)
- **FACT (high confidence):** In an August 27, 2026 8-K, Rivian said McDonough notified it on August 24 that she would resign effective October 30, 2026; it said the resignation was not the result of disagreement on financial reporting, operations, policies, or practices. Rivian expected Vice President of Finance Derek Mulvey to become interim CFO on her departure while it conducted an executive search. At the cut-off, that appointment was prospective, not yet an established fact. [August 2026 CFO-transition 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000110465926102435/tm2624125d1_8k.htm)
- **FACT (high confidence):** The 2026 proxy lists seven directors: Scaringe, Peter Krawiec, Sanford Schwartz, Karen Boone, Aidan Gomez, Jay Flatley, and John Krafcik. The company reported that Boone and Gomez were elected at the June 2026 annual meeting. [2026 proxy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000023/rivn-20260427.htm) · [June 2026 voting-results 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000045/rivn-20260622.htm)
- **FACT (high confidence):** Rivian has Class A and Class B common stock; Class B has ten votes per share and, according to the Q2 10-Q, all Class B shares are scheduled automatically to convert to Class A in November 2026, subject to the charter's earlier conversion triggers. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Note 14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000054/rivn-20260630.htm)
- **INFERENCE (medium confidence):** The announced CFO transition is a near-term continuity risk, though the record gives a planned interim arrangement and no reporting-related disagreement. The scheduled sunset of the dual-class structure may change voting influence; it does not itself predict a governance outcome.

## 7. Legal, regulatory, and safety matters

- **FACT (high confidence):** The Q2 10-Q says the court gave final approval in May 2026 to a $250m settlement of consolidated IPO-related securities class actions, with an aggregate $181m net settlement payment by the company after insurance recoveries. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Note 13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000054/rivn-20260630.htm)
- **FACT (high confidence):** At June 30, Rivian reported approximately $60m accrued for estimated contingent losses and said it considered losses up to about $90m reasonably possible, i.e. up to about $30m above that accrual. The filing describes the accrual as covering, among other things, supply-contract and legal-proceeding exposures; it is not a case-by-case damages estimate. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Note 13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000054/rivn-20260630.htm)
- **FACT (high confidence):** The same filing reports a consolidated stockholder derivative action, *In re Rivian Automotive, Inc. Stockholder Litigation*, Delaware Chancery Court case 2024-0127-MTZ, stayed through August 27, 2026 by an order dated July 16, 2026. Rivian says it does not believe the final outcome of pending matters is likely to have a material adverse effect, while acknowledging outcomes cannot be predicted with certainty. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, legal proceedings](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1874178/000187417826000054/rivn-20260630.htm)
- **FACT (high confidence):** NHTSA's recall acknowledgement for campaign 26V009 states that Rivian Automotive, LLC recalled certain 2022–2025 R1T and 2022–2026 R1S vehicles. This report does not infer a defect rate, causal finding, or financial impact from the acknowledgement alone. [NHTSA recall acknowledgement](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2026/RCAK-26V009-9580.pdf)
- **INFERENCE (medium confidence):** Litigation, contingent-loss exposure, and recalls are material diligence subjects, but the disclosures do not support a conclusion that any one matter threatens solvency or establishes wrongdoing. The final settlement of the IPO case reduces uncertainty for that matter; remaining matters still require monitoring.
- **UNCERTAINTY (high confidence):** This is not a complete court-docket, regulator-enforcement, warranty, or recall-history search. Confirm current case status, insurance recoveries, recall remedy completion, and any non-US proceedings through counsel and the relevant primary databases.

## 10. Open questions and next evidence to obtain

1. **Q3 financial results:** Rivian scheduled release for October 29, after the cut-off. Obtain the filed 10-Q/earnings release before updating revenue, margins, cash, debt, and guidance.
2. **CFO succession:** Did McDonough leave on the announced date, did Mulvey become interim CFO, and who was ultimately appointed? Obtain a subsequent 8-K.
3. **R2 economics and demand:** Obtain production cost, deliveries, pricing/mix, warranty, order/cancellation, and capacity evidence rather than relying on launch or guidance statements.
4. **Funding conditions:** Check whether DOE and Volkswagen-JV loan conditions were satisfied, the amounts drawn, collateral/covenants, and effects on liquidity and leverage.
5. **Legal and recall status:** Verify court dockets, any new suits/enforcement matters, final payments/insurance, and recall population/remedy completion.
6. **Sanctions / ownership:** Conduct a dated, auditable screening of the correct legal entities, relevant people, and ownership/control relationships; preserve query parameters and results.
7. **Related-party / counterparty exposure:** Assess Amazon, Volkswagen, Uber, suppliers, and joint-venture arrangements against the actual proposed transaction and most recent filings.

## Method and limitations

This refresh prioritized issuer-filed SEC records and U.S. government sources. SEC filings are representations by the issuer, with audited status indicated where relevant; they do not independently prove every business claim. The October 2 production release is furnished as an SEC exhibit and contains the company's forward-looking guidance. No paid databases, social-media sentiment analysis, comprehensive litigation docket search, site visit, interviews, credit-agency file, or full sanctions/PEP/adverse-media screen was performed. Absence of an item from this report is not evidence that it does not exist.
