## 1. Question restated

Is Agent First Opening worth building and launching within 14 days on IMD or CLAUS, and, if it is, what is the smallest version that proves it?

## 2. Verdict in five lines

1. **Gap: partly served.** **Record (read 2026-10-23):** launch products already offer decaying sniper taxes, wallet caps and uniform-price auctions; none of the reviewed live products proves one allocation per independent agent identity. [Pons v2](https://docs.ponsfamily.com/v2), [Flaunch API](https://docs.flaunch.gg/references/api), [Uniswap CCA](https://github.com/Uniswap/continuous-clearing-auction/blob/main/README.md).
2. **Speed: 14 days for a falsification MVP on IMD Sepolia; no public launch in that window.** **Inference (2026-10-23):** the current evidence does not clear identity farming, router coverage or production hook admission. CLAUS cannot replay its opening and its extension owner has no timelock. [CLAUS host verified source](https://sourcify.dev/server/v2/contract/1/0xea0436465676eA7001535Bfe2E5cbd4b137b7f91?fields=all), [Uniswap hook routing](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/protocols/v4/concepts/hook-routing).
3. **MVP:** **Inference (2026-10-23):** an immutable IMD Sepolia v4 hook admits signed, preapproved seat-owner or agent-wallet EOA transactions for the first 120 blocks, caps the *actual token output* per identity, then becomes an ordinary 1.25% pool; three independently owned agent reviewers post eligibility decisions and receive onchain escrow bounties after verification. No ZK in this experiment.
4. **Largest failure risk:** **Record and Inference (read 2026-10-23):** ERC-8004 registration is permissionless and IMD seats have concentrated ownership; even an origin-bound identity signature does not prove a unique human or the eventual router recipient. Smart-account and relayer flow is unresolved. [ERC-8004 specification](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8004), [IMD seat owners](https://api.imd.fun/seats/owners), [v4 router interface](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-periphery/blob/main/src/interfaces/IV4Router.sol).
5. **Total IMD cost:** **Inference (2026-10-23):** three MVP requests × 0.5 IMD = **1.5 IMD** before gas and agent bounties: two `job.open` and one `launch.open`; a later optional oracle adds **0.5 IMD**. [IMD docs](https://imd.fun/docs/), [capabilities](https://api.imd.fun/requests/capabilities).

## 4. Hook spec

**Deployment target (Record, read 2026-10-23):** IMD supports `univ4_hook` launch requests on Ethereum and Robinhood Chain and a Sepolia test chain, accepts a `customToken` only when transfer behavior is ordinary and supply cannot be minted after launch, and takes 10% of supply for its swarm. Its Ethereum LaunchFactory source at verified address [`0x12c63b581d07093f6126bc02263c58f7eadaa96f`](https://sourcify.dev/server/v2/contract/1/0x12c63b581d07093f6126bc02263c58f7eadaa96f?fields=all) deploys, initializes and seeds in one transaction, requires `beforeInitialize` or `afterInitialize`, and currently uses a 1.25% pool fee for launches. [IMD docs](https://imd.fun/docs/), [capabilities](https://api.imd.fun/requests/capabilities). The candidate token uses IMD's plain fixed-supply ERC-20. No transfer fee, transfer restriction, venue lock, or post-launch mint exists.

**Callbacks and flags (Inference design, 2026-10-23):** `beforeInitialize` (`1 << 13`) requires the immutable, verified IMD factory as its `sender`, binds one pool key, validates the configured token pair/fee and stores `endBlock = block.number + 120`; no other caller may bind the hook first. `beforeSwap` (`1 << 7`) checks opening buy eligibility and nonce, and returns a **positive specified-currency** input premium as a `BeforeSwapDelta`; `beforeSwapReturnsDelta` (`1 << 3`) is therefore required. `afterSwap` (`1 << 6`) checks the realized token output and updates `filled[identityKey]`. No other callback or permission flag. The address low-bit mask is `0x20c8`; mine a CREATE2 salt for that mask. A return-delta flag without its callback is invalid in v4. [v4 Hooks library](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/libraries/Hooks.sol), [IHooks interface](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/interfaces/IHooks.sol), [PoolManager accounting](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/PoolManager.sol).

**Opening condition and data (Inference design, 2026-10-23):** only exact-input token buys at `block.number < endBlock` require nonempty `hookData`; sells remain open. The ABI tuple is `(uint8 version, uint8 identityKind, uint256 identityId, bytes32 canonicalIdentityKey, address snapshotSigner, address submitter, uint256 maxInput, uint256 nonce, uint64 deadline, bytes32[] allowlistProof, bytes signature)`. The Merkle leaf is `keccak256(abi.encode(originChainId, registryAddress, identityKind, identityId, canonicalIdentityKey, snapshotSigner))`. An EIP-712 permit commits to `chainId`, hook, `PoolId`, `sender` (the calling router), `submitter`, those fields and the fixed opening cap; `abs(amountSpecified) ≤ maxInput`, deadline and unused nonce are checked before swap. The **smallest MVP admits only identity-controlled EOAs**: `submitter == tx.origin == snapshotSigner`, that EOA signs, and `snapshotSigner` must equal the current `ownerOf(seatId)` or nonzero ERC-8004 `getAgentWallet(agentId)` on the chain with the registry. A seat/agent-wallet transfer after the snapshot invalidates this opening proof; token transfers remain unrestricted. Binding both router and origin prevents another EOA from copying an exposed permit into its own transaction. A prelaunch immutable Merkle root marks approved keys; an ERC-8004 registration **alone never qualifies**. A reviewer must consolidate a seat and a linked agent ID to one canonical key. Every spent nonce and each key's cumulative realized output persist across blocks and wallets. Empty data works after opening. On Sepolia, use a root containing Ethereum seat ownership at a pinned Ethereum block; the open-mint Sepolia test seat NFT cannot establish scarce identity. Fork tests separately exercise live Ethereum registry calls. Smart-contract wallets, account-abstraction bundlers and relayers are excluded *during the opening* until a recipient-verifying route is proven; this is an account-path limitation, not a token transfer or venue lock. [ERC-8004 EIP](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8004), [official registry deployments](https://github.com/erc-8004/erc-8004-contracts), [IMD test-chain contracts](https://imd.fun/docs/), [v4 router interface](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-periphery/blob/main/src/interfaces/IV4Router.sol).

**Router truth (Record and limitation, read 2026-10-23):** v4 passes `PoolManager.swap` its immediate caller as `sender`; a Universal Router or other intermediary is therefore visible instead of the trading wallet. The v4 router carries `hookData` per swap hop, but its swap action and later token-settlement actions can direct output independently. A hook can verify that an identity EOA *submitted and authorized* the purchase by checking `tx.origin` plus its EIP-712 signature; it cannot prove the token-recipient wallet or beneficial funder from arbitrary router calldata. `tx.origin` is intentionally used only for this EOA-only opening path, not as admin authority. The MVP must describe access this way. It must accept any router that forwards valid `hookData` for an identity EOA; nonforwarding routes revert only in the opening. Uniswap routing may additionally require hook allowlisting. Support in 0x, 1inch and other aggregators is **not found**. [PoolManager](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/PoolManager.sol), [V4Router](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-periphery/blob/main/src/V4Router.sol), [Uniswap hook allowlist](https://developers.uniswap.org/hook-allowlist), [routing guidance](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/protocols/v4/concepts/hook-routing).

**Fee math (Inference proposal, 2026-10-23):** retain the IMD static LP fee at 12,500 parts per million (1.25%). `beforeSwap` takes a flat 2,500 ppm (0.25%) of *gross input* during the opening and zero thereafter, rounded down. PoolManager credits the hook's custom-accounting balance; a permissionless sweep can take only to the immutable fee vault. Neither rate can rise. For an eligible exact-input **1 ETH** buy during opening: hook premium `1 × 0.0025 = 0.0025 ETH`; the pool sees `0.9975 ETH`; LP fee `0.9975 × 0.0125 = 0.01246875 ETH`; pre-price-swap input `0.9975 − 0.01246875 = 0.98503125 ETH`; aggregate fee `0.01496875 ETH`, or **1.496875%** of gross. After opening: pool fee `1 × 0.0125 = 0.0125 ETH`, pre-price-swap input `0.9875 ETH`. Price impact is additional, not a fee. The verified IMD LP split allocates 80% of LP fees to the requester recipient and 20% to the network. The proposed requester recipient is an immutable, opt-in staking vault: ordinary token holders may deposit/withdraw freely; its pull-based two-currency accounting sends **80%** of requester LP fees and the opening premium to stakers, and at most **20%** of requester LP fees repays the sponsor to a fixed cap. Premium accumulated before anyone stakes streams over a fixed day after opening rather than being captured by the first depositor. No ERC-20 transfer rule changes. The custom-accounting arithmetic, reward accounting and factory admission are **untested** MVP gates. [v4 Hooks library](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/libraries/Hooks.sol), [PoolManager](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/PoolManager.sol), [IMD factory](https://sourcify.dev/server/v2/contract/1/0x12c63b581d07093f6126bc02263c58f7eadaa96f?fields=all), [IMD docs](https://imd.fun/docs/).

**Fairness and invariants (Inference design, 2026-10-23):** three buys in one private bundle still share `filled[key]`; `filled[key] + actualTokensOut ≤ cap` after each swap, regardless of router or nonce. The cap is a token quantity fixed before launch, not an input quote vulnerable to price movement. `afterSwap` reverts the whole fill if it would exceed the cap; a later transaction can choose a smaller exact-input amount. Equal identities still face ordering and price impact, so this is allocation fairness rather than equal execution price. No privileged creator or agent exemption. At `endBlock` every buy and sell uses ordinary v4 routing with no proof, and the premium is permanently zero. The hook cannot later raise fees, extend the window, change the root, or move the cap. The vault has fixed distribution rules and sponsor payout cap; staker recipients vary only by voluntary deposits, with no hook/vault admin key or proxy. IMD's separate `PoolFees.setRequester` lets the *current requester* redirect future LP fees immediately, so the vault must be installed as requester before the first open swap and must have no function to forward that call; otherwise the no-admin rule is unmet. Test token ordering, opposite swap direction, flash routing, split bundles, exact-output rejection during opening, rejection of EIP-1271 signers, nonce reuse, replay across pools/chains and arithmetic overflow. [IMD verified PoolFees source within LaunchFactory bundle](https://sourcify.dev/server/v2/contract/1/0x12c63b581d07093f6126bc02263c58f7eadaa96f?fields=all).

## 5. Agent spec

**What the agents do (Inference design, 2026-10-23):** before launch, three independently owned IMD-seat agents inspect candidate seat ownership, ERC-8004 identity metadata, onchain funding/transfer clusters and prior accepted-work evidence. They use judgment to flag likely common control and publish a short evidence hash plus signed yes/no decisions for each candidate. Graph hints can be produced by ordinary code; deciding whether ambiguous profiles and work histories establish independent agents is the AI task. Agents submit their decision Merkle roots and evidence content hashes onchain to a nonupgradeable `ReviewEscrow` with three immutable reviewer EOA addresses and an Ethereum snapshot hash. On Sepolia, the escrow verifies their signatures, matching-root quorum, duplicate-owner/equivocation challenges and a fixed delay, then pays each valid reviewer from a prefunded balance. Ethereum ownership and WorkRegistry claims in the root are checked on a pinned Ethereum fork before the sponsor funds it; **Sepolia cannot directly verify that other chain's state**. A challenger can demonstrate two included leaves with the same owner and different keys, or conflicting signed roots; the escrow withholds the disputed bounty. **Limitation:** quorum and these mechanical checks cannot prove independence of humans, the truth of every Ethereum receipt, or the quality of subjective analysis, so the root remains a trusted-reviewer choice. This is the core economic experiment, not a trustless Sybil cure. [IMD docs](https://imd.fun/docs/), [ERC-8004 EIP](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8004).

**Who signs what (Inference design, 2026-10-23):** each pinned reviewer seat owner signs an EIP-712 `(chainId, escrow, snapshotBlock, candidateRoot, evidenceHash, expiry)` attestation and posts it onchain. The eligible EOA seat owner or registered EOA `agentWallet` signs each swap permit described in §4 and must be the transaction origin. The hook checks signature, origin, root proof, nonce and cap before any fill. The escrow checks reviewer signatures, two matching roots from the three immutable reviewer EOAs and onchain-provable duplicate/equivocation predicates, waits a fixed **24-hour** challenge period, then pays ETH to each signer; it cannot check whether the AI's inference or an Ethereum-only receipt is true. The hook never pays a reviewer per trade, so reviewers have no incentive to approve extra volume. A missing or contested root prevents opening launch; it does not trigger an emergency admin override.

**Oracle question (Inference, 2026-10-23):** **no `oracle.request` in the smallest MVP.** If the escrow test succeeds, a separate optional check can ask one chain-local Boolean: “At Ethereum block **N**, did every ERC-8004 ID in the named candidate list have at least one non-revoked `NewFeedback` entry from the IMD WorkRegistry client, yes or no?” `N`, the candidate list, the WorkRegistry client and the exact feedback predicate must be fixed in the paid request; do not submit a vague question. IMD panels return one EIP-712 signature, checkable onchain, but the panel signer remains trusted and responses can take longer than opening blocks. The onchain escrow is the payment gate; an oracle response is corroboration, not the per-buy admission path. No oracle scheduling is used for the opening: official minima are **10 minutes** for oracles and **30 minutes** for jobs, far slower than the first blocks. [IMD docs](https://imd.fun/docs/), [capabilities](https://api.imd.fun/requests/capabilities).

**Identity-farming measurement (Record, read 2026-10-23):** the [ERC-8004 EIP](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8004) permits anyone to register identities and warns that reputation needs a trusted client context. One owner (`0x28556846b891f349817f8731ad85cae5800acd86`) registered five consecutive Ethereum IDs in blocks **26,254,241–26,254,293** on **2026-10-23**: [first](https://etherscan.io/tx/0xfd6965a3e4d1f1d092f86ef509bcac50bf9141850154b1c10f71457069b7895b), [second](https://etherscan.io/tx/0xe504a65650b0d8623cc97243e0e56c974de73c6c9ed11002ef364263ed3c832e), [third](https://etherscan.io/tx/0xa6adbcb75e3543b695ae95b88afb92d091b72ac78691c8a2bf5ca276ec12fd3e), [fourth](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x8bc4cfbf76dbec43eda9de580319994ca14df70d8aa8e222a4a9ac0483a15746), [fifth](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x3400eacfe1c8cc0293c565903af406cc170af592aa11556f57d620d6bf0497f2). Each successful registration used **598,347 gas**; effective gas prices were **56,213,970**, **60,524,348**, **60,342,648**, **55,147,275**, and **56,806,117 wei/gas**. Total observed fee = `598,347 × (56,213,970 + 60,524,348 + 60,342,648 + 55,147,275 + 56,806,117) / 10^18 = 0.000172942841006226 ETH`, or **0.0000345885682012452 ETH per ID** on average. This excludes metadata hosting and any future reputation-building cost.

[IMD seat-owner snapshot](https://api.imd.fun/seats/owners) at **2026-10-23 19:45:14 UTC** reported **2,000 seats**, **788 owner addresses**, and a largest owner holding **77**; a seat is scarcer but still not one human. [ERC-8004 contracts](https://github.com/erc-8004/erc-8004-contracts), [IMD docs](https://imd.fun/docs/).

**Failure modes (Inference, 2026-10-23):** colluding reviewers, bought or transferred seats, a single actor funding multiple agent wallets, false metadata, delayed or disputed reputation batches, signature replay, expired permits, and an oracle signer outage. Revoke a compromised root only *before* immutable hook deployment; after deployment there is no key and no rescue path. A failed root means do not launch. ZK nullifiers are excluded: they would conceal the buying wallet only after a credential issuer has already established independence, and would add proof cost without removing the trusted reviewer. [ERC-8004 EIP](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8004), [IMD docs](https://imd.fun/docs/).

## 6. Economics

**Day-one payer (Inference, 2026-10-23):** the launch sponsor funds IMD requests, deployment gas and an ETH `ReviewEscrow` before reviewer work. The buyer pays the normal pool fee and a small opening-only premium. The sponsor pays because the experiment seeks a defensible initial holder distribution; agents do not bear launch risk. The bounty is paid onchain from escrow after verification, independent of IMD's offchain compensation or EIP-712 oracle signature. Sepolia test ETH proves payout mechanics but is not economic compensation; a later public launch needs a real-ETH bounty accepted by the reviewers before they work. If no sponsor prefunds, this version should not launch.

**Recorded unit costs (read 2026-10-23):** [IMD capabilities](https://api.imd.fun/requests/capabilities) and [docs](https://imd.fun/docs/) price `job.open`, `oracle.request` and `launch.open` at **0.5 IMD each** on Ethereum/Robinhood Intake. Two jobs plus launch = `3 × 0.5 = 1.5 IMD`; a subsequent oracle = `4 × 0.5 = 2.0 IMD` cumulative. The **10%** swarm supply share is a token allocation, not **0.5 IMD** cash. Gas, hook salt mining, reviewer bounties and legal/operational costs have no verified quote here. **Record (read 2026-10-23):** IMD's current pool LP fee is **1.25%** and the requester/network division is **80/20** of that fee, or `1.25% × 0.8 = 1.00%` and `1.25% × 0.2 = 0.25%` of fee-bearing input before other v4 accounting. [Verified IMD LaunchFactory](https://sourcify.dev/server/v2/contract/1/0x12c63b581d07093f6126bc02263c58f7eadaa96f?fields=all), [IMD docs](https://imd.fun/docs/).

**Proposed split and break even (Inference, 2026-10-23):** route the requester fee to an immutable vault: **80%** of that share to opt-in token stakers and **20%** to repay sponsor costs until a fixed cap, then **100%** to stakers. Thus at current LP rates, the sponsor's maximum fee recovery is `1.00% × 0.20 = 0.20%` of fee-bearing trading input and holder reward is at least `1.00% × 0.80 = 0.80%`; the network keeps **0.25%**. The opening **0.25%** premium goes to holder rewards, never to the sponsor, so it cannot bias sponsor incentives to prolong the opening. Let `C_ETH` be actual sponsor gas + escrow bounties + the **1.5 IMD** MVP requests converted at the *paid* execution price, and `V_ETH` be total fee-bearing input measured in ETH equivalents. Cost recovery needs `0.002 × V_ETH ≥ C_ETH`, so `V_ETH ≥ C_ETH / 0.002 = 500 × C_ETH`; this omits price impact, changing fee bases and vault gas. Since no IMD/ETH price or launch volume is verified, a numeric break-even volume is **not found**. The fee design rewards holders while capping total opening fee near **1.5%** rather than a 99% sniper tax, but volume retention remains an experiment. [IMD docs](https://imd.fun/docs/), [Pons v2](https://docs.ponsfamily.com/v2).

## 7. Home fit

**IMD (Record and Inference, read 2026-10-23):** the platform already offers a custom v4-hook launch on Ethereum/Robinhood, a test chain, fixed-supply token rules, seat owners and paid work. Its [swarm API](https://api.imd.fun/swarm) reported **1,026 agents online**, **1,055 enrolled seats**, **69 jobs** and **16 oracles** completed in the preceding day at **2026-10-23 19:54 UTC**; this is an API snapshot, not an independently audited labor count. The [Ethereum LaunchFactory](https://sourcify.dev/server/v2/contract/1/0x12c63b581d07093f6126bc02263c58f7eadaa96f?fields=all) was Sourcify-verified **2026-10-08**; [Intake `0xa43e6f75ee006411f79ac1c84120606c2330de82`](https://imd.fun/docs/) is documented on chains **1** and **4663**. IMD must accept this specific hook permission mask and `beforeSwap` accounting, disclose/test the fee-recipient handoff, allow a Sepolia hook launch, and supply the sponsor with actual deployment parameters. A concrete no-admin path is a prefunded, immutable vault that submits the fixed `launch.open` request itself and is recorded as the initial requester; whether IMD accepts a contract payer as requester is **not verified**. The sponsor must commission/prefund review. IMD's `LaunchFees` owner can change the *network* fee recipient without a timelock, though the existing pool fee and fixed **80/20** LP split cannot be changed through it; strict interpretation of the no-admin rule therefore needs IMD to constrain that external control too. If the factory refuses the mask, fee accounting or recipient handoff, stop; there is no compliant workaround via token transfers or a venue lock. ## 10. Kill criteria

**Measurable gates (Inference, set 2026-10-23):**

1. **Identity economics:** in a documented exercise, if a single funding/owner cluster can obtain **two or more** opening allocations for a cost below the value of one allocation, kill the mainnet proposal. Record gas and actual cap value at the test snapshot; a bare ERC-8004 registration already fails the identity standard. [ERC-8004 EIP](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8004).
2. **Accounting and bundle safety:** any Foundry fuzz/fork counterexample with `filled[key] > cap`, opening premium above **2,500 ppm**, a post-opening premium above **zero**, a sell blocked by the gate, or a fee-vault shortfall kills this hook implementation. [v4 core](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/PoolManager.sol).
3. **Route availability:** if fewer than **two** independently controlled transaction paths pass valid hookData and produce a normal user quote on Sepolia, or if a required integrator does not allowlist the hook, kill a public launch. Direct v4 router plus a custom transaction builder count as only **one** independent path. [Uniswap allowlist](https://developers.uniswap.org/hook-allowlist).
4. **Agent payment and independence:** if three distinct seat-owner addresses cannot submit eligible decisions, the escrow fails to pay all valid reviewers after its fixed delay, an independent Ethereum-fork check finds any false receipt claimed in a paid root, or the reviewers will not accept the sponsor's posted real-ETH bounty terms for production, kill the “agent” claim. Beneficial independence remains an unresolved audit item even if addresses differ.
5. **Home admission:** if IMD factory rejects the hook address, delta accounting, fee recipient or Sepolia launch, kill this deployment rather than adding a transfer tax or privileged router. [IMD factory](https://sourcify.dev/server/v2/contract/1/0x12c63b581d07093f6126bc02263c58f7eadaa96f?fields=all).

## 11. Moonshot

**Boldest compliant version (Inference, 2026-10-23):** make every launch on IMD use a private, portable one-per-independent-agent credential whose zero-knowledge nullifier is bound to a single pool and opening window. The proof conceals the registry ID and its link to the buying wallet while the hook checks a nullifier and cap; the router or settlement transaction may still expose the wallet itself. A separate threshold of economically bonded AI reviewers signs credential eligibility and is paid by an onchain escrow for evidence-backed decisions. The token remains freely transferable, the pool becomes ordinary after the opening, and the hook/vault have no admin. This ZK layer removes the trusted *linking service* that otherwise learns both identity and buying wallet; it does **not** remove trust in credential issuance or reviewer judgment. [ERC-8004 EIP](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8004), [v4 Hooks library](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/libraries/Hooks.sol).

**Smallest falsifiable experiment in 14 days (Inference, 2026-10-23):** on a local Foundry fork, issue **three** real registry-backed credentials to distinct test agents, make **two** wallets per agent produce proofs, then show one accepted opening buy per agent, a second-wallet rejection with the same nullifier, no public link from swap calldata to registry ID, and a reviewer payout onchain after a valid decision. Measure prover time, proof calldata/gas and a deliberately duplicated credential. The sponsor pays prover/build gas and prefunds reviewer ETH bounties on day one; proposed pool fees can reimburse only after real volume. No chain launch is required for this falsification.

**Why it might be a bad idea (Inference, 2026-10-23):** ZK can hide only the wallet link, while an attacker can still farm ERC-8004 IDs or control multiple seats and receive multiple valid credentials. The trusted reviewers become a bottleneck and may collude; proof generation adds latency exactly when a launch is most time sensitive; opaque eligibility and missing aggregator support may exclude ordinary buyers. CCA's equal-price auction or a simple time-decaying fee may supply enough practical fairness without identity issuance, reviewer disputes or a bespoke cryptographic circuit. [ERC-8004 EIP](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8004), [CCA README](https://github.com/Uniswap/continuous-clearing-auction/blob/main/README.md), [Uniswap hook routing](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/protocols/v4/concepts/hook-routing).
