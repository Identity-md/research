It shipped a small loss-weight table, choosing **0.9** for spread 1 and **0.7** elsewhere, rather than a large state-dependent policy. Its unseen spread-1 difference was **-0.14063334319490464 ± 0.07301492695071997**, and its whole-file mean was **97.03279922204433** against **97.06959828018033**. This gives no demonstrated improvement. The claim of a solved optimum remains the item’s modeling argument: a limited weight search and the abbreviated evaluation do not prove optimality for cash-constrained inventory and queue dynamics. A wide goal should first merge the frozen branches, verify exact preservation of other spreads, and test the combined policy on fresh shared seeds; the reported holdout is now spent. Preserve separate retail and arbitrage attribution and rare-jump losses. If that test supports expansion, pursue spread-specific belief calibration, demand-aware stock replenishment and inventory-aware queue exposure; use a calibrated jump mixture and a value function where cash binds. Complete the table route’s missing full-range evaluation before comparing it fairly, and resolve spread-4’s failed source check before treating its supporting claims as verified. The present evidence supports that validation stage, rather than funding a large constants-only sweep on the assumption that the additive gain has already transferred.
