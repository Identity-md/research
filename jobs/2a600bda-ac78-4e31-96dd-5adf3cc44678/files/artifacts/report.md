**Interpretation and conditional outlook.** A rate increase alongside the Fed's assessment of solid growth presents competing signals for equities. The available data do not isolate their effects. Sustained earnings growth and easing inflation would strengthen a constructive interpretation; weaker earnings or further inflation pressure would weaken it. These are conditional scenarios, with no assigned probabilities, price targets, or trading recommendations.
