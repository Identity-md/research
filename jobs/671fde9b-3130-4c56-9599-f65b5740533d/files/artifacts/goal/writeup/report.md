<a id="target"></a>

**What has to be proved.** In the sources' notation, let `m = x1`, `n = x2`, and let `R = x3` be a decidable bipartite relation with no `K₂,₂`: two distinct left vertices and two distinct right vertices cannot have all four cross edges. The function is

```text
F(z) = OR over (i,j) in R of (z(left i) AND z(right j)).
```

For every circuit `C = x5` satisfying the project's `IsAO2 (m+n)` predicate and computing `F`, the target requires a circuit `D = x9` computing the same function, with `D.size ≤ C.size`, and sets of wires `x10`, `x11`. Wires in `x10` must be left inputs or outputs of OR gates whose operands are also in `x10`; `x11` has the corresponding right-input condition. Every AND gate of `D` must have operands `[a,b]` with `a ∈ x10` and `b ∈ x11`, in that order. The condition covers every AND gate, including gates outside the output's dependency cone. This is a paraphrase, not a replacement formal statement. [A2, notes §§1, 5(D)](#a2), [A3, “WHAT THE STEP SAYS”](#a3); the target conclusion is also visible in the pinned [R_meta_2976_41 source](https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/blob/e125f04d0dc6fed67cee48a1d06692912e167c32/lean/PEqualNP/Outside/R_meta_2976_41.lean).

All four sources identify the same central obstacle. Write `E` for the finite set of edges of `R` and `τ(R)` for its minimum vertex-cover size. For nonempty `E`, they identify the optimum gate count in the required form as

```text
B = |E| + τ(R) - 1.
```

Their argument is that a surviving product `(OR S) AND (OR T)` must have `S × T ⊆ R`. The absence of `K₂,₂` makes it a star. A minimum vertex cover supplies a star construction; the sources' reverse count prices the leaf ORs, the star ANDs and the ORs combining the stars. A3 gives the hand derivation, and A1/A4 point to project results for construction and counting circuits already in the required form. This is the sources' reduction of the problem, not a newly checked general lower bound. [A1, notes §1](#a1), [A2, notes §1](#a2), [A3, “REDUCTION”](#a3), [A4, notes §1](#a4).

The missing claim is that **every** allowed circuit computing this function already has at least `B` gates. A4 gives a matching formulation: for every matching `M ⊆ E`,

```text
|E| + |M| ≤ C.size + 1.
```

A4 describes obtaining the cover bound through König's theorem, then building the oriented star circuit. No input proves the bound for arbitrary `IsAO2` circuits. For the empty relation, the sources instead describe a circuit with no gates and output wire `m+n`, which evaluates to false under the project's semantics; this also covers an empty side of the relation. [A1, notes §§1–3](#a1), [A2, notes §5(E)](#a2), [A3, missing lemma and reduction](#a3), [A4, notes §§1, 3 and final paragraph](#a4).
