**How the selected files keep the queue lead.** At a fixed tick, the simulator sorts by placement sequence. Depletion schedules the rival's replacement on the opposite side, separated by its spread; the replacement receives a new sequence. An order already resting at that destination can therefore precede it. Arbitrage consumes the entire mispriced quantity, making a larger front order vulnerable to a price jump. Those mechanics support preserving useful earlier orders while controlling exposure.
