The method changed during the run. The plan records six changes and says they were chosen from whole-field measurements, without examining how individual entrants would move:

1. Claude and Codex scores were mapped onto a common curve after their high-score rates differed.
2. The second read, deep field and final panel were widened to the sizes above.
3. Deep seats added rankings; combined score and head-to-head standing replaced score alone for qualification.
4. The pull toward the field mean was based on actual second-read persistence instead of reference-plugin agreement.
5. Missing first reads received fresh seats and a route into the deep round.
6. Entries still owed a qualifying second read received deep seats instead.

The straggler rule allowed progression at nine in ten accepted after the rest ran over 30 minutes; deep required 55 of 60 or 150 minutes, and final ten of twelve. Recovery and extra deep work had 40- and 60-minute limits. Slow first- and second-read items eventually landed and their evidence counted, without redealing earlier rounds. The process was adaptive; the explanation that changes used whole-field numbers comes from the plan. [PLAN]
