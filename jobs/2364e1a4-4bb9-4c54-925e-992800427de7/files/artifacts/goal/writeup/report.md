# Five images for "meadow hikari transformator tempus portafoglio", and the one to deliver

**Deliver `nocturne-coil`, titled "The Hours We Keep" (`nocturne-coil.svg`).** Its complete image source is in Appendix B and can be saved as a file as it stands. It is the only one of the five images that all three reviewers placed in their top two, and the only one where every word of the prompt is something you can point at.

## The sampling

Five workers each drew one original image from the exact prompt "meadow hikari transformator tempus portafoglio", each under a different artistic direction. Every image is a 2048 x 2048 SVG vector illustration with no linked images, fonts, scripts or web addresses, carried whole in the `svg` field of its result. Three other workers then each reviewed all five, independently and from different angles.

I rendered all five in headless Chromium at 2048, 1024 and 256 px, with no load errors, and looked at each one. The reviewers rendered with resvg-js, so two different renderers have drawn the images correctly.

## Why `nocturne-coil` is the best choice for this group

- **Every prompt word is visible.** Grasses are the meadow. The moon and its beam are hikari, Japanese for light. The copper coil is the transformator. The tick-marked orbits and the seed that has already rooted are tempus, time. The stitched wallet is portafoglio, Italian for wallet. Anyone shown the prompt can trace each word into the picture.
- **It holds up close.** At full size the coil windings carry highlight stripes, the moon has wave bands, the wallet has dashed stitching and two embossed leaf shapes, and each falling leaf is drawn separately. I saw all of these in my own render.
- **It is interesting to talk about.** One event runs from top to bottom: light goes in, living things come out and a wallet keeps them. What is being saved (light, growth or time) is left open.
- **It looks like nothing else in the set.** The engraved-almanac treatment, with its frame, corner marks and dotted orbits, appears in no other candidate.
- **It survives small sizes.** At 256 px the moon, coil and wallet still read as one stack, which matters wherever the NFT is shown as a thumbnail.

Its weaknesses are real and none is a rendering defect:

- Two reviewers found the centered, symmetric stack poster-like.
- The bottom third is dark and the grasses are busy.
- The finest tick marks and the sprout's roots disappear at 256 px.
- The navy edges may blend into a dark page.
- The wallet-and-seedling story is fairly literal, which leaves less room for five different readings.

One reviewer suggested lightening the wallet and lower grasses, and another suggested strengthening the tick marks and roots. I did not commission that revision: the image is delivered as drawn and as reviewed.

## The closest alternatives

**`woven-hours`** is the only other image a reviewer placed first. Five bands gathering in one shared pouch is the most direct picture of five people making one thing, and its cream ground is the lightest palette of the five. It loses because light, transformation and time are only implied: all three reviewers gave it 3 for prompt fit. Its weave texture also flattens at small sizes. One of its scores needs a caveat. The `visual-craft` review describes four bands and sprigs in the corners, but the image has five bands, each ending in a sprig, so that review's 73 may understate it for a group of five. If the group cares more about a symbol of shared authorship than about seeing the prompt, this is the image to switch to.

**`prismatic-herbarium`** is second on mean score and has the strongest thumbnail, with 5 for thumbnail readability from all three reviewers. It loses on meadow and time. Its flowers ride on shards instead of growing in a field, time shows only as leaf, bloom and falling petal, and two reviewers pointed to its large empty ultramarine areas.

`dawn-fold` is calm and pleasant, but its pages read as a plant and its look is the most familiar. `orbital-garden` covers every prompt word at full size, but at 256 px it turns into a dark green blur.

## Limits

- **Nobody in the group was asked.** The scores and this choice are editorial judgments by three reviewers and me. They are not votes by the five recipients and say nothing about resale value.
- **Uniqueness is not guaranteed.** Each image is an original drawing made for this goal, but nobody searched for similar existing works.
- **The NFT tool is unknown.** The goal does not name it, so its upload formats were not tested. Nothing was minted.
- **These are drawn vector illustrations, not text-to-image model outputs.** The goal named no generator and bought no media orders, so each worker drew one SVG from the prompt.
- **Only the chosen image is here in full.** This report is capped at 64,000 bytes and one full SVG takes about 45,000. The other four are complete in the results above and appear here only in the preview.
