# L-meta-4021 (desk pequalnp): write-up of four results

**Outcome: the step is not proved.** The goal asks for a Lean 4 theorem proving L-meta-4021, checked by another holder's machine, and then a pull request on open-goat/pequalnp-proofs. None of the three exists. All four supplied results have `"status": "missing_lemma"` and say that no Lean file is offered ([angle 1](#angle-1), [angle 2](#angle-2), [angle 3](#angle-3), [angle 4](#angle-4)). So there was nothing for a second holder to check and no pull request to open. This report does not claim a proof, a check or a PR URL, and it makes none up. The goal stays open until someone proves the missing lemma described below, or the requester accepts that the step is open.

## What the results establish

- **The statement is well formed.** Lean 4.36.0 with the prebuilt project elaborates it with a `sorry` body ([angle 1](#angle-1), [angle 2](#angle-2)). Angle 3 elaborated nothing.
- **The statement.** f is the point-line incidence function of PG(2,3) on 13+13 inputs (difference set {0,1,3,9}, 52 edges). Take any fan-in-2 AND/OR circuit for f with at most 64 gates and distinct operands that has a nested AND pair and a dirty AND gate. The claim is that it has an enabled path from wire 13 to the output at e_0, or from wire 0 at e_13 ([angle 1](#angle-1), [angle 4](#angle-4)).
- **The size bound is essential.** Each result builds a circuit that meets every hypothesis except the size bound and breaks the conclusion. The four counterexamples have different sizes:
  - 68 gates, 5 nested pairs ([angle 1](#angle-1))
  - 67 gates, 13 nested pairs ([angle 2](#angle-2))
  - 66 gates, or 65 without the dirty-gate hypothesis ([angle 3](#angle-3))
  - 66 gates, andPairs = 12, with properties also checked by `#eval` on the project definitions ([angle 4](#angle-4))

  The authors checked these with their own scripts over all 2^26 inputs, not in Lean. The standard circuit has exactly 64 gates ([angle 3](#angle-3)).
- **The step implies an unconditional lower bound.** Put a small gadget in front of or behind any circuit for f. If the step holds, every IsAO2/Nodup circuit for f then has at least 61 gates ([angle 1](#angle-1), [angle 4](#angle-4)). Angle 2 gets at least 62 gates ([angle 2](#angle-2)). Angle 1 argues this on pen and paper, not formalised.
- **The project has no such bound.** At brilliant-hq/pequalnp-proofs e125f04, every 64-gate lower bound found carries a side condition:
  - no nested AND pair: L_meta_4803, L_meta_1751, L_meta_2059, L_meta_5158
  - andPairs = 1: L_meta_5162
  - top form with empty junk: L_meta_8791
  - line degree 1: L_meta_2420, L_meta_5254

  All four results say they searched by grep and did not read every file.
- **Existing reductions.** R_meta_4021_47 and R_meta_4021_487 derive the step from two hypotheses ([angle 3](#angle-3)):
  - (i) a generic flip-tracing lemma (`locate`/`trace`), described as a routine induction that nobody has written yet;
  - (ii) the size-specific residue (`collision_case`/`bound`). This is the missing lemma: it needs C.size >= 65 for circuits with the collision structure ([angle 2](#angle-2)).
- **Supporting lemmas.** L_meta_4019 reduces every flag to (0,13). The clean-case method of L_meta_2069 does not carry over when a dirty AND is present ([angle 2](#angle-2), [angle 4](#angle-4)).
- **Small cases (angle 4).** An exhaustive search covered nine small graphs:
  - The minimum sizes are 3,4,3,5,5,4,5,5,5, which equals edges + vertex cover - 1.
  - At minimum size no circuit has a junk AND.
  - At +1 gate, P4 has 220 eligible circuits, all satisfying the conclusion.
  - At +2 gates the conclusion fails for the 2-matching (140 of 2452) and for P4 (1268 of 75308).
  - K_{1,3} first fails at +3 gates (3561 of 873216).

  These results come from [angle 4](#angle-4).

## What could not be found

- No proof of the missing lemma. Nobody has written the trace lemma either.
- No counterexample at 64 gates or fewer.
- No second-holder check.
- No pull request.

Whether the statement is true at 64 or 65 gates is undecided ([angle 4](#angle-4)).

**Suggested next step** ([angle 3](#angle-3)): post `collision_case` from R_meta_4021_487 and `locate` as separate steps. With both proved, R_meta_4021_487 closes L-meta-4021 unchanged.
