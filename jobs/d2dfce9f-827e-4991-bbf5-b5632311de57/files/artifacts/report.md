## Counting rule

A person would count only with evidence of both (1) a current affiliation with a named organization and (2) their account following @surfcoderepeat, in that direction, at an identified observation date. Count unique people once across both groups. For this report, affiliation means a current staff, contractor, leadership, or board role; former staff and general ecosystem contributors would be listed separately. Organization accounts would not count as people. These are methodological assumptions, not supplied definitions.

“Ethereum Labs” was not resolved to a specific official website, X handle, or staff roster by the searches below. It has not been silently substituted with Ethereum Classic Labs, a different company containing “Labs,” or the wider Ethereum ecosystem. Clarifying this entity is necessary for an exhaustive combined count.

## Method and interpretation

Research used public web search, direct attempts to open X, and inspection of returned public profile material. Searches included `"surfcoderepeat" Ethereum`, `"surfcoderepeat" followers`, `"surfcoderepeat" "Ethereum Foundation" followers`, `"surfcoderepeat" "follows" "ethereum"`, `"surfcoderepeat" "followed by"`, `"surfcoderepeat" "ivangbi_"`, and `"Ethereum Labs" team`. These searches returned no usable complete follower-and-affiliation dataset. No authenticated X follower export was available or retrieved.

**Facts about the investigation:** direct X retrieval failed; third-party material supplied some profile and relationship claims; no qualifying person was verified.

**Inference:** the retrieved evidence is insufficient to determine the requested count. Search results are not an exhaustive follower census, so the absence of verified matches cannot justify a zero population count.

**Uncertainty:** follower relationships and employment change over time; mirrors may be stale; pseudonymous accounts may not map cleanly to unique people; the intended Ethereum Labs entity and the user's desired treatment of former employees or grantees are unspecified. No confidence interval is offered because no representative sample or complete population was obtained.

3. Match each candidate to current organizational or first-person affiliation evidence, recording name, handle, organization, role, affiliation URL, follow evidence, and observation date. Deduplicate people with multiple accounts or affiliations.
4. Report the verified names and union count. If coverage remains partial, publish only a supported minimum and explicitly label it partial.

This is a limited research result, not an independently audited follower census. Local document checks establish file integrity only; they do not certify source truth.
