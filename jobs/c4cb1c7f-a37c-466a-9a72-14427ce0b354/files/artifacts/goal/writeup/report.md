**What measurement found.** true-mean measured 99.23869339150623 on seeds 40000-43999, with retail 127.07987834308194, arbitrage -27.8411849515945, top-decile share 0.5265243835574145 and estimated standard error 12.37313011835025 for a 200-run mean. draw-variance did not finish its planned 30 x 200 study: its 14 x 200 block means ranged 77.5 to 112.6, and top 20 of 200 contributed 51.6% of edge. Settlement and spread therefore complicate unpaired rankings. oracle-bound's +1.05 for retail parameters versus +8.78 for the next jump flag and +24.34 for next-step probability point toward quote exposure, but are policy-specific hidden-information diagnostics, not attainable gains or global bounds. demand-fit changes policy as well as estimating demand, so its results do not settle that comparison. leaders-gap and tick1-jump-cost show that arbitrage savings can also remove retail profits; rich-runs' expanded ask capacity likewise failed on holdout. [M1: true-mean, draw-variance, oracle-bound, leaders-gap, tick1-jump-cost, demand-fit][M1] [M2: rich-runs][M2]

**Which combinations added up.** combine-cash reports paired holdout gain 2.083619011192334 with standard error 0.4067546948632805, the strongest combination evidence. combine-late-game and combine-three lack their own holdout base controls, so their absolute scores do not establish paired gains. Overflow yields 0.2832087540598333 with standard error 0.3651905827107454; voting yields 0.056340704598667295 with standard error 0.23682551783950723. combine-two-cent's gain 0.036431343287000005 uses its own holdout base 96.00139032710622, explaining why it is positive despite scoring below the common control. Belief replacement loses -1.0599375611583355 with standard error 0.49740626976596103; pair ultimately disables latent belief, and the six-way sequence fails to exceed the common base holdout. Five of six earlier constants failed to transfer; all six together lost 1.87 a run. Separate gains cannot simply be added. [M1: combine-cash, combine-late-game, combine-three, combine-overflow, combine-vote, combine-two-cent, combine-belief, combine-pair, combine-all, combine-constants][M1]

**What fresh starts found.** Useful additions beyond the base are censored demand learning for stock targets (fresh-fingerprints), fill-based retail-intensity estimation (fresh-rules-lawyer), and recognizing FIFO priority acquired after submission (fresh-time); the last reports only 0.02733549288983328 paired holdout gain. FIFO chunking in fresh-gifts yields 0.036612788687167254 with standard error 0.2817268065582916, not an established improvement. fresh-free and fresh-opposite rediscovered retention already in the base; fresh-free finally retunes constants, while fresh-opposite and fresh-literal return the base, the latter after holdout-based selection. fresh-competitor has only a two-seed smoke test supporting its graft, not the full ranges its fields label. fresh-tail returns the base after incomplete candidate evaluation and reports different ranges. fresh-solve supplied no strategy after an xcrun runtime failure; wildcard-2 supplied none after download failures. wildcard-1 independently supports demand-learning research, but neither oracle experiments nor public fingerprints prove an executable gain or another entrant's mechanism. [M2: findings for all fresh items, wildcard-1 and wildcard-2][M2]

**What could be taken away.** simplify removes secondary purchases and dead controls while retaining probes, beliefs and queue logic; it also changes the stock-entry gate, general risk to 0.9 and the stock-cash cap, so its gain cannot be attributed solely to deletion. Code shrinks from 13882 to 9972 bytes. Its logged paired holdout gain is 1.6671149102061666 with standard error 1.1776597053936184; its approach instead writes 1.6671149102061662. arb-budget also removes secondary stock but misses its joint target: holdout retail is 119.517920457476 and arbitrage -22.0396719198325. ablation-map finds overlapping stock and spread-rule effects: joint stock deletion costs 24.3924403209095, so marginal effects cannot be summed. Last-step and other-ammunition removal intervals include zero; inactive pre/nb1/gs cleanup is reported neutral. no-probes bundles removal with compensating estimator changes yet loses -8.29 +-0.76 on holdout. fewer-cancels' +0.05 +-0.72 does not establish that longer retention helps. Keep the information and cheap-stock core while separately testing secondary-purchase removal. [M2: simplify, arb-budget, no-probes, fewer-cancels][M2] [M1: ablation-map][M1]

**Where the next wave should start.** Start from combine-cash, with simplify as an independent alternative and the unchanged base as paired control. Assign separate tests to purchase removal and its entry gate, demand fitting, demand-matched crossing cover, late-game stock rules and near-tick controls; combine only after independent replication because inventory and collateral effects overlap. cross-anywhere's higher-tick acquisition and tick2-queue's queue commitment deserve separate validation. Freeze policies before a fresh untouched holdout, use paired common-seed differences with uncertainty and tail sensitivity, and preserve the intended settlement/spread distribution: the shared holdout is exposed. No public submission or first-place result is established.
